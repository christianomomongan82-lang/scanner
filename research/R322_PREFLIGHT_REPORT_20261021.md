# R322 USD high-impact macro-release cascade — pre-outcome gate report

Date: 2026-10-21  
**Binary verdict: GAGAL — DATA/SAMPLE.** The registered study terminated before realized trade outcomes were computed.

## What was tested

The locked hypothesis was that an unusually large first five-minute EURUSD move after a scheduled high-impact USD macro release may continue after a further five-minute manual-entry delay. The entry and exit rules, event family, 1.5× impulse threshold, 60-change baseline, exact T+10 entry, continuation-at-delay filter, risk geometry, cost stresses, period splits and sample/robustness gates were committed in [the R322 preregistration](R322_NEWS_CASCADE_PREREGISTRATION.md) before any PnL was calculated. The calendar timestamp correction was separately registered in Amendment A before this event-alignment preflight.

Research motivation was a distinct news-flow mechanism, not another variation of prior price-only breakouts or mean reversion. Literature has discussed exchange-rate responses to macroeconomic announcements through microstructure and stop-loss order flow; see the 2011 article, DOI [10.1016/j.intfin.2010.09.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.intfin.2010.09.001). The ISM official calendar places Manufacturing and Services PMI at 10:00 ET, while BLS, BEA and Census schedules place the registered employment/inflation/retail/GDP releases at their specified release clocks; links are listed in the preregistration.

## Calendar timestamp integrity

The source calendar snapshot contained 83,427 rows, filtered into 2,828 USD high-impact rows before event-name selection. The source CSV SHA-256 was `f4e92bca4168cfe6e4598d3d2a69ac933dfd6c7c94550e056498a418e3efaf5e`; the filtered input SHA-256 was `6fb6481fa4b8cb83b67461d20db757689bf77df2818122fa2a32a2d148e59ebd`.

Because many known scheduled releases were stored with a midnight placeholder time, the preregistered correction retained their source-local calendar date and substituted only the locked official Eastern release time, with IANA DST conversion. Non-midnight entries were retained only when their converted Eastern time agreed with the registered schedule within ±15 minutes. All 2,828 source rows were audited:
- 1,199 midnight times replaced by their locked schedule;
- 544 original offset-aware timestamps validated against the schedule;
- 107 non-midnight schedule conflicts excluded;
- 1 source/Eastern calendar-date mismatch excluded;
- 977 rows outside the allowlist excluded.
This left 1,743 event rows clustered into 973 episodes: 359 discovery episodes, 167 confirmation episodes and 447 contaminated diagnostic episodes. The corrected time and episode lists are reproducibly generated by the committed calendar preparation/correction code; the data-only Actions run is [39601019683](https://github.com/christianomomongan82-lang/scanner/actions/runs/39601019683). It never computes signals or PnL.

## Disposition

**R322 = GAGAL — DATA/SAMPLE.** This verdict means the locked event universe plus five-minute manual delay and confirmation filter cannot satisfy the minimum sample size; it is not a claim that news is never predictive. No PnL, PF or challenge-pass probability was calculated. No 2026 data was scored. Do not widen event names, relax the trigger, alter the clock, invert direction or shorten the delay as a post-hoc rescue. A new news hypothesis would need a materially distinct economic justification and a fresh preregistration before outcome computation.
