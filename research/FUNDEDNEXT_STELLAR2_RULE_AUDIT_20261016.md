# FundedNext Stellar 2-Step — Official Rule Check

Checked: 2026-10-16  
Purpose: constrain R189 research and purchasing plans; public help-center rules can change.

## Verified on official FundedNext pages

- **Minimum age: 18.** The official age-restriction FAQ says a trader must be at least 18 to open an account. Do not purchase or attempt KYC before eligibility.
- **Smallest listed Stellar 2-Step:** $6,000, approximately $59.99 before promo/add-ons.
- **Targets:** Phase 1 8%; Phase 2 5%.
- **Challenge risk limits:** Daily Loss Limit 5% and Maximum Loss Limit 10%, each calculated against initial account balance/equity per the model FAQ.
- **Minimum trading days:** five separate calendar days in each phase by default. The Challenge page lists a “No Minimum Trading Days” add-on for the $6k/$15k/$25k accounts; its price is not published in the overview and must be checked at checkout before comparing total cost.
- **Time limit:** no challenge phase time limit shown on the current Stellar 2-Step page.
- **Quick Strike:** a position closed within 30 seconds counts; the percentage is the share of total recorded profit generated by profitable trades closed within 30 seconds, per cycle. On challenge, 20% produces a warning and 30% or above holds progression until the ratio drops below 30%; do not interpret this as the percentage of trades.
- **Inactivity:** latest official CFD FAQ dated July 27, 2026 says 30 consecutive calendar days without a trade deactivates the account—not 60 days.
- **Server time:** FundedNext's loss-limit FAQ says server time follows a seasonal adjustment: GMT+3 during daylight saving time, GMT+2 otherwise. The exact mapping used by the user's actual login/platform still must be verified in the platform.
- **EAs:** current Stellar 2-Step overview permits EAs and custom indicators on sizes under $50,000 subject to the account's settings. This does not affect the current plan to execute manually by phone.
