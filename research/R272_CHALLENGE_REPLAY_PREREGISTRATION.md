## Official rule values and source
See `FUNDEDNEXT_STELLAR2_RULES_VERIFIED_20261016.md` for links and the current public Stellar 2-Step interpretation:
- Reference initial balance: $6,000 in each phase.
- Phase 1 closed-P&L target: +$480 (balance $6,480).
- Phase 2 closed-P&L target: +$300 from a fresh $6,000 phase balance.
- Daily loss: $300 (5% of initial phase balance). Current-day closed P&L plus floating P&L across open positions, including commission/swap/fees, must stay above −$300; reset at 00:00 server time.
- Maximum loss: static $5,400 equity/balance floor (10% of initial phase balance). Floating drawdown counts.
- At least five distinct trading days in each phase. No official time limit.
- Quick Strike: P&L from positions closed in under 30 seconds must remain strictly below 30% of positive profitable P&L. The project's safer internal threshold is **below 20% upper-bound profit share** because this is the warning area in the public rules.

## Data replay / execution
- Use only the R272 trade stream generated by the locked rules, with the observed BID/ASK fill side and the five-minute delayed entry. Do not replace it with ideal-entry fills.
- Use the +$0.30/oz additional transaction cost as primary and +$0.50/oz as stress; when simulating daily equity, split the additional round-trip cost 50/50 between entry and exit. Actual account-specific commission, spread add-ons, swaps and fees are separate verification items; unknown costs are not treated as zero.
- Position sizing for every trade: dollar stop risk = current phase realized equity × predeclared risk percent; dollar P&L = risk dollars × trade R, where R already includes observed executable side and the chosen incremental cost.
- No overlapping positions. R272 is forced flat by 16:45 New York time, before the 00:00 seasonal GMT+2/GMT+3 server reset; verify this timing against the account platform before any live use.
- Reconstruct mark-to-market adverse excursion from each M5 OHLC path with the same BID/ASK exit side and conservative stop-first intrabar rule. If an OHLC bar hits the stop, use the stop fill; if the open gaps through the stop, use the worse open fill. Never credit a price beyond observed OHLC. Daily-loss checks use the day's closed P&L plus the open trade's mark-to-market P&L after costs. The static floor is checked against equity including floating P&L.
- Quick Strike is measured in money P&L, not R. Because M5 data cannot locate an exit to the second, count **all profitable trades exiting in the entry M5 bar** as a conservative upper bound on sub-30-second profit, and divide their path-specific positive dollar profit by all positive profitable P&L in that phase. If this upper-bound share is at or above 20%, the candidate fails the internal buffer gate; if M5 data cannot substantiate the calculation, do not claim funded-ready.
- Minimum trading days are distinct server calendar days with at least one entry. The actual seasonal server clock must be applied to timestamps; use Europe/Helsinki only as a *provisional GMT+2/GMT+3 mapping* until compared with actual account-platform timestamps.

## Fixed risk grid and path method
- Test **0.25%, 0.50%, 0.75%, 1.00%** fractional risk per trade. No other risk values may be added after seeing outcomes.
- 10,000 two-phase path simulations per risk value, fixed seed `2722026`.
- Use 4-week circular blocks of the locked 2026 OOS daily trade/no-trade stream to preserve clustering. Build sequential paths to at most 180 trading days for both phases combined; this is an internal speed-evaluation horizon, not a firm-imposed time limit. Do not use an IID trade shuffle as the primary estimate.
- Each simulated phase begins with $6,000 and its own daily-loss counter / trading-day count; Phase 2 starts a new $6,000 phase balance and fresh static/daily loss counters after Phase 1 succeeds. Continue the same resampled sequence for Phase 2, preserving the order within each selected week block. Pass only when the phase's **closed** P&L reaches its target, at least five separate server trading days have been traded, Quick Strike is clear under the internal buffer, and no risk breach has occurred.
- Daily-loss breach: at every intratrade M5 checkpoint, if that server day's closed P&L plus open floating P&L/charges is ≤−$300, the path fails. Maximum-loss breach: if any realized or floating equity is ≤$5,400 in that phase, the path fails. No negative P&L may be ignored because a trade later recovers.
- If target and loss breach occur on the same M5 candle without known tick ordering, assume loss breach first. If the 2026 OOS stream has too few complete four-week blocks for inference, gate fails; don't fill this evidence gap with discovery returns.

## Project-level success gates (stricter than firm rules)
A risk value can be selected only if all mandatory conditions are met:
1. Estimated probability of completing **both** phases before any loss-rule breach is at least 70%.
2. At least 90% of paths terminate without an unmodelled loss-rule violation; all simulated outcomes, including paths that fail the targets by day 180, remain in the denominator.
3. Median time to finish both phases is ≤90 trading days and the 90th percentile ≤180 trading days.
4. Both the +$0.30/oz primary and +$0.50/oz cost stress keep two-phase completion probability ≥70% (no risk retuning between cost cases).
5. Quick Strike upper-bound share is <20% in each phase for the promoted risk setting, and at least five distinct trading days are satisfied in both phases.
6. The lower 95% uncertainty bound for both-phase completion probability is reported using path-level bootstrap/binomial uncertainty; a point estimate alone is not accepted.
7. No high-risk lottery selection: report breach odds and time-to-target beside pass odds for every risk value. If none passes all criteria, `R272 = GAGAL FOR FUNDED READINESS`.

## Non-negotiable limitation
Even if the replay clears its project gates, it is not an unconditional guarantee and remains simulation-only until actual platform server-time/reset semantics, symbol specification, commission/swaps/fees, execution/slippage and legal eligibility are verified. A 5-minute bar cannot validate exact under-30-second exit timing; the Quick Strike upper bound is a conservative filter, not a tick-exact measurement.
