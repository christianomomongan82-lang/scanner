# True Project Objective: Sustainable UMK-Level Income Without a Conventional Job

Date: 2026-10-23

## The real objective

The user's underlying life objective is not merely to pass a prop-firm evaluation. It is to establish reliable income equivalent to the Manado municipal minimum wage without depending on a conventional job.

For 2026, public copies/listings of the North Sulawesi Governor's decision identify UMK Manado as **Rp4,022,017 per month** (Governor's Decision No. 407/2025, effective from 1 January 2026). Reconfirm the official decision when necessary.

Operational interpretation: prioritize **cash actually received and available to use**, not unrealized account equity, simulated P&L, advertised account size, or a single lucky payout. Unless the user specifies otherwise, judge the income target on a net / take-home basis after profit split, fees, and other applicable costs.

The phrase “without having to work” must not be blurred:
- Income without a conventional job is different from passive income that requires virtually no ongoing labor.
- Manually trading a prop account still requires research, decisions, monitoring, and execution; it is not truly passive.
- A prop-firm payout is conditional on program rules, performance, verification, and the provider's reward process. It is not a guaranteed salary.

The best route should be selected from evidence, not assumed to be trading simply because this project began as a backtest project.

## Recommended scorecard

1. **Primary outcome:** net, withdrawable non-employment income at least Rp4,022,017 in each month, rather than an average distorted by one exceptional month.
2. **Sustainability evidence:** aim to verify the target across six consecutive months, tracking zero-payout months, drawdown, failed accounts, fees, time spent, and any dependence on additional spending. This six-month verification window is a proposed measurement standard, not a user-imposed deadline.
3. **Risk:** no strategy may be called successful if the payout path depends on a material likelihood of catastrophic loss, prohibited execution, or unsustainable risk.
4. **Labor requirement:** record time/effort needed. If the income still requires continuous manual work, label it self-employment/trading income—not passive income.
5. **Intermediate milestones:** passing FundedNext Stellar 2-Step within day 179 is an intermediate milestone, not the life objective. A passed challenge without real, compliant, withdrawable rewards does not satisfy the primary goal.

- No strategy in the current project has yet been established as funded-ready; R329 failed its economic confirmation screen.
- Consequently, the current evidence does not justify treating FundedNext as a dependable near-term income source.
- On a nominal $6,000 account, a simple illustrative calculation using USD/IDR = Rp16,000 and the standard 80% reward share requires roughly 5.24% account growth in a month to generate Rp4,022,017 before any additional taxes or costs. This is arithmetic, not a claim that the return is sustainable or attainable.
- FundedNext's published eligibility terms require the account holder to be at least 18. Do not purchase or attempt to open/use an account before eligible; do not use another person's identity. Use the waiting period for research and verified simulation only.
- Do not assume a funded account is a live $6,000 capital account or that simulated results guarantee reward payments. Re-check current program terms and payout requirements before spending money.

## Decision principle

Do not force every solution through trading. Compare candidate routes—including prop trading, creating a small digital asset/business, and income-producing capital where feasible—by startup cost, time to first actual cash, probability of reaching the monthly target, downside risk, ongoing labor, and repeatability. With the user's limited budget and phone-only constraint, reject options that secretly require expensive tools, large capital, or substantial upfront fees.

For backtesting specifically, work only on questions that can materially improve the probability of reaching the true income objective. Use independent internet evidence and data when useful; don't keep generating strategy variants merely to produce another experiment. If the best supported conclusion is that the current funded-trading route is not viable, say so and compare alternatives.
